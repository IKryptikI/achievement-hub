Become a mapper

Mappers are the reason achievements unlock. They play a game with record mode on and find the exact moment each achievement happens, so it unlocks automatically for everyone after them.

🎮 You play

Turn on "Help map achievements" in the app. When you earn something, press A in the overlay.

🔎 It learns

Record mode works out what changed in the game. A second run confirms it.

🏆 Everyone earns

Your mapping goes into the database with your name on it, plus a Mapper badge.

It's completely optional: nobody's game is watched unless they turn record mode on themselves. Fill this in and we'll get in touch.
