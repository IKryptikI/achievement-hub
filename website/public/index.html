Why this exists

Plenty of great PC games came out before achievements existed, or lost them when services like Games for Windows Live shut down. Their console versions often had full lists. Achievement Hub brings those back to the PC, tracks them while you play, and lets you show them off to your friends.

Install the app

It finds your games wherever they live: Steam, GOG, Heroic, Bottles, a folder on a USB drive.

Add a game

One click puts the overlay in the game. No launch options, no mod manager. Launch it however you usually do.

Play and pop

Achievements pop in-game. Press Shift+F2 or the Xbox button for your list, your friends and your games.
