Privacy

Last updated October 2026. Plain language: here's exactly what we keep.

Your account

  • Gamertag, password and email. Your password is never stored, only a salted, slow hash of it. Same for your recovery code. Your email is private: it is only used to verify your account, reset your password and answer mapper applications, and those emails are sent through our email provider (Resend). We never send marketing.
  • Profile details you choose to add: gamer picture, color, motto, bio, favorite game, banner and showcase.
  • A login cookie so you stay signed in (30 days). No advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics.

Your game progress

  • Only if you link the app to your account. Then it sends your Gamerscore, games with progress, which achievements you've unlocked, your recent unlocks and the game you're playing.
  • It never sends anything else from your computer: no files, no game list beyond supported games, no hardware details.
  • "Only friends can see my games" in your account hides your progress from everyone except friends.

Record mode (mapping)

Record mode is off unless you turn it on. When on, the overlay watches the memory of the game it's running in (only that game) to learn when achievements happen. What it learns stays on your computer unless you choose to share a mapping.

Mapper applications

If you apply to be a mapper, we keep what you send (name, email, Discord, answers) to contact you about mapping, and nothing else.

Where it's stored

On Cloudflare's servers (the site and its database). Game lists and covers are loaded from GitHub and the stores and databases credited on the About page.

Deleting your data

Account → Delete account removes your account, profile, progress, friends and tips immediately. To delete a mapper application, email the address on the FAQ page.
